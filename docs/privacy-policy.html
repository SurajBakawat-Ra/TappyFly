Privacy Policy for TappyFly

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Solara Games ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Android game TappyFly (package com.solaragames.tappyfly, the "Game"). By installing and playing the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, postal address or phone number inside the Game.

The Game and the third-party services listed below may automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size, app version, and a resettable advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID).
  • Usage and gameplay information: game sessions, levels reached, scores, in-app events (for example when an ad is shown or a purchase is made), and crash/diagnostic logs.
  • Approximate location: coarse location inferred from your IP address, used to serve region-appropriate advertising. We do not collect precise GPS location.
  • Purchase information: if you buy an in-app item, Google Play processes the payment. We receive only a purchase confirmation and product identifier — never your card details.
  • Google Play Games profile (optional): if you sign in to Google Play Games Services, we receive your player ID and display name in order to show leaderboards and achievements.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game, save your progress and deliver the features you use.
  • To display advertising that keeps the Game free, including personalised advertising where you have consented.
  • To measure ad performance and understand how the Game is used so we can improve it.
  • To detect and fix crashes, abuse and fraud.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.

3. Third-party services

The Game uses the following SDKs. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — policies.google.com/privacy, How Google uses data
  • AppLovin (advertising) — applovin.com/privacy
  • Vungle / Liftoff (advertising) — vungle.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services, Google Play Games Services and Google Play Billing — policies.google.com/privacy

These providers may combine the information they collect with information from other apps or websites, and may transfer it to countries outside your own. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices and controls

  • Personalised ads: you can opt out or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. You can also choose "No to all" on the in-game advertising consent screen at any time.
  • Ad settings: adssettings.google.com
  • Deleting your data: the Game stores progress only on your device; uninstalling it removes that data. To request deletion of any data a third-party provider holds about you, email us at the address below and we will forward the request to the relevant provider, or use that provider's own deletion tools.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.

6. Data retention and security

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. Data transmitted by the SDKs above is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. International transfers

Our third-party providers may process data in countries other than the one you live in. Where required, they rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
